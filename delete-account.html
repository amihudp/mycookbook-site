Deleting your MyCookBook account

In the app: Settings → My account → Delete account. It happens at once and cannot be undone.

Without the app: email mycookbookservice@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with "account deletion" in the subject. We verify the request comes from the account holder, delete, and confirm by reply — within 30 days, usually far sooner.

Deleted: the account and its sign-in, uploaded recipes and photos, comments, likes and ratings, follows, notifications and push tokens. Kept: recipes saved only on your own phone, which were never sent to us — removing the app removes those too. A record that you accepted the terms (version and date only) may be kept briefly as legal evidence, and encrypted backups are erased within 30 days.
